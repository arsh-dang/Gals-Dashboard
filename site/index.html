Provider view — participation and outcomes across STEM activities for school-age girls, reshaped from the GALS programme survey. Built for funder and programme-level reporting.

1Participation overview

How many girls took part in each activity?

Respondent counts by activity type.

Every respondent in this sample took part in GALS — it will always be the largest bar and is shown in a neutral colour for that reason. The other bars show additional programmes attended alongside it; they are not a comparison against GALS. A general, non-activity-specific outcomes question in the source data has been excluded from all four views (see note below).

Where do school-student respondents come from?

Respondent count by region and school year — school students only.

Cells with fewer than respondents are suppressed and marked, not silently dropped or shown as zero. Geelong is the only region with usable numbers across most year levels — treat any other region's row as indicative at best.

2Outcomes by activity

Which outcomes do participants report most positively, by activity?

Average score per activity, on the survey's 1–4 scale. 1 = Yes a lot is the best answer; "I do not know" is excluded from every average.

"≈ wording variant" marks three outcome statements that appear twice with near-identical wording (a survey artifact — one activity's question block used slightly different phrasing than the rest). They are shown as separate rows for now, adjacent to their pair, so the pattern is visible rather than looking like missing data. "×" marks an activity/outcome combination with fewer than respondents in the current filter — suppressed, not plotted.

3Skills and identity

What skills do participants say they built, by activity?

Multi-select — respondents could tick more than one, so these are frequencies, not averages. Percentage of that activity's participants who selected each item.

Only activities with a skills/identity battery in the survey are shown here — three activities (Excursions, Online STEM activities, and the excluded general-outcomes rows) never asked these questions, which is a survey branch, not missing data.

4Regional comparison

How do outcomes compare across the six regions?

Average score per region, same 1–4 scale as above (1 = Yes a lot is best; "I do not know" excluded).

"×" marks a region/outcome combination with fewer than respondents — suppressed, not plotted. Most regions outside Geelong will show several suppressed cells; that is the honest state of this sample, not a rendering gap.

Data notes

  • Source: respondents.csv, activity_ratings.csv, battery_selections.csv from the reshaped Qualtrics export. open_text.csv was not present in this data drop and is not used.
  • A generic, non-activity-specific outcomes question (134 responses, reshaped as activity_type "Other") is excluded from every view — it is not a real 8th programme.
  • The scale runs 1 = Yes a lot → 4 = No, with "I do not know" at position 5. Lower is better; every average shown here is inverted for display and labelled.
  • Cells under 5 respondents are suppressed and visibly marked throughout, never silently dropped or averaged.
  • Colours are drawn from a placeholder palette (site/css/tokens.css) pending Deakin's official brand palette — replace before this is shown outside the project.
