2Outcomes by activity

Which outcomes do participants report most positively, by activity?

Average score per activity, on the survey's 1–4 scale. 4 = Yes a lot is the best answer; "I do not know" is excluded from every average.

The faint line through each dot is a 95% confidence interval — the activity types sit close together, and without it the differences between them look more certain than the sample size supports. Hover a dot for the exact interval and respondent count. "≈ wording variant" marks three outcome statements that appear twice with near-identical wording (a survey artifact — one activity's question block used slightly different phrasing than the rest). They are shown as separate rows for now, adjacent to their pair, so the pattern is visible rather than looking like missing data. "×" marks an activity/outcome combination with fewer than respondents in the current filter — suppressed, not plotted.

3Skills and identity

What skills do participants say they built, by activity?

Multi-select — respondents could tick more than one, so these are frequencies, not averages. Percentage of that activity's participants who selected each item.

Only activities with a skills/identity battery in the survey are shown here — Excursions, Online STEM activities, and the general-outcomes block never asked these questions, which is a survey branch, not missing data.

Data notes

  • Source: respondents.csv and activity_ratings.csv from the reshaped Qualtrics export. battery_selections.csv and open_text.csv are not present in this data drop, so the views that depend on them show an explicit empty state rather than stale or invented data.
  • "General STEM outcomes (not activity-specific)" is a real question block asked of everyone, shown on its own in the Outcomes section — it is not a real 8th activity, so it's excluded from every activity and region comparison.
  • The scale runs 1 = No → 4 = Yes a lot, with "I do not know" tracked separately rather than as a fifth scale position. Higher is better and every average is used directly, with no inversion.
  • Cells under 5 respondents are suppressed and visibly marked throughout, never silently dropped or averaged.
  • Colours follow the real Deakin Dashboard Style Guide (Nov 2025) — see site/css/tokens.css for sourcing notes.
