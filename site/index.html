2Outcomes by activity

Which outcomes do participants report most positively, by activity?

Average score per activity on the survey's 1–4 scale (4 = Yes a lot is best; "I do not know" excluded).

Faint lines are 95% confidence intervals: activities sit close together, so read a gap alongside its interval, not instead of it. "≈" flags an item that appears twice with near-identical wording; "×" marks a cell suppressed for fewer than respondents.

General STEM outcomes (not activity-specific)

5Aspirations and subject choice

Does taking part in GALS shift how girls see their future in STEM?

Compares GALS participants to everyone else, item by item, on the same scale as Outcomes above.

"Diff" is the GALS average minus the everyone-else average; in this mock data it's small and mixed in direction, which is expected. "×" marks a group/item suppressed for fewer than respondents.

What helps students decide which subjects to choose?

Compares which influences matter more to female versus male students. Each bar splits into a female and male segment, sized to that gender's own percentage (multi-select, so these don't sum to 100%).

Mock data only, not a result - and since every GALS participant is female, part of any gender gap here is a GALS-participation gap. A segment under respondents shows "suppressed".

What helps students decide what they might do in the future?

Compares which influences on future plans matter more to female versus male students. A separate question from subject choice above; segment length is each gender's own percentage (multi-select).

Mock data only, not a result - female includes all GALS participants, so part of the gap reflects programme participation. A segment under respondents shows "suppressed".
