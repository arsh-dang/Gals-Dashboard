Is your cohort's average response typical of the overall sample?

Average score per outcome statement, your school (●) against all respondents (◆), on the survey's 1–4 scale. 1 = Yes a lot is the best answer; "I do not know" is excluded. This collapses across activity type — use the detail above for an activity-by-activity view. Closed by default: your own cohort's detail is the point of this page, not how it stacks up against the programme.

"×" marks an outcome statement with fewer than of your students answering — suppressed, not plotted. The diamond (all respondents) is shown even where your school's dot is suppressed, since it draws on the full sample. The faint line through each mark is a 95% confidence interval — with cohort sizes this small, treat any gap from the diamond as indicative, not conclusive.

Data notes

  • School cohorts in this mock dataset are small — most are well under the suppression threshold. Real collection may look different; this view is a first pass pending actual teacher feedback on what's useful.
  • One respondent's raw school and school_level values in the source CSV contradicted the data contract (populated on a Post-school row, which should be blank) — both are treated as blank here rather than shown, per the contract's own rule.
  • Colours are drawn from a placeholder palette (site/css/tokens.css) pending Deakin's official brand palette.
